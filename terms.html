Playing safely

Redemptio is a wildlife rescue game. Its creatures, encounters, habitats, rewards, and restoration are virtual. Do not approach or feed wild animals, trespass, or travel to unsafe places to play. Pause gameplay when crossing roads, cycling, or driving, and supervise children outdoors.

Steps and game information

Step-based rewards are optional game features. They are not medical advice, a fitness prescription, or a promise of health outcomes. Maps, weather, species ranges, and conservation facts are provided for gameplay and learning; they are not navigation, emergency, or scientific survey tools.

Purchases and saves

The current game has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Resources and Seeds are virtual game items. Saves are local and can be lost when deleting the app or device data; the game does not offer an account-based recovery service.

Third-party services

Maps and weather depend on external services and may be temporarily unavailable. See credits and service links and our privacy policy. For support, contact rodrigoqsandler@gmail.com.
