Accessibility

Redemptio includes accessibility labels in parts of its interface and some behavior that responds to Reduce Motion. We have not yet verified every gameplay task with assistive technologies on every supported device, so we do not claim full VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, or other system-wide accessibility support.

Map exploration and rescue throws use visual and touch-based interactions. If an interaction prevents you from playing, contact rodrigoqsandler@gmail.com with the screen and action involved, the assistive technology you use, and your device model. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis or medical history.

This support website uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible links, responsive layouts, and selectable text. It does not include animation or autoplaying media.

Support and privacy contact: Rodrigo Sandler · rodrigoqsandler@gmail.com
